Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how SpareQuote (“SpareQuote,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) may collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit our website or use our spare-parts discovery and supplier quote-request services. It should be read alongside any notices presented in the product.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on the features used and how the service is implemented, information may include:

  • Contact and account details: name, email address, phone number, business name, and account credentials if account features are offered.
  • Part and quote details: part names or numbers, specifications, quantities, compatibility information, delivery preferences, messages to suppliers, and quote responses.
  • Supplier and business information: business contact details and product or availability information supplied by users or participating suppliers.
  • Technical information: device, browser, diagnostic, and access-log information generated when the site or hosting infrastructure is used.
  • Support communications: information included when contacting us.

Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information unless a feature specifically requires it and provides an appropriate notice.

3. How information may be used

Information may be used to provide and maintain the service; help identify relevant parts; transmit quote requests and responses; communicate with users and suppliers; provide support; protect against fraud, abuse, and security incidents; troubleshoot and improve performance; and meet legal obligations. We will not use information for a materially different purpose without an appropriate basis or notice.

4. When information may be shared

To operate a quote-request service, relevant request details may need to be shared with suppliers you choose to contact. Information may also be processed by providers supporting hosting, communications, security, or other operational functions, subject to appropriate arrangements where required. We may disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and service integrity.

The actual practices of the deployed product must be reviewed and this statement updated if applicable law or product integrations require more specific disclosures.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

The current landing-page files are designed without first-party analytics or advertising scripts. The hosting provider or future integrations may process technical data or use cookies and similar technologies. If analytics, advertising, authentication, embedded content, or other third-party services are added, update this policy and any required consent controls.

6. Retention

Information should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, resolve disputes, maintain security, and meet legal requirements. Actual retention periods depend on data types and systems; define and publish specific periods once the production architecture is known.

7. Security

We aim to use reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational measures to protect information. No website, transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Access should be limited to authorised people and service providers who need it for legitimate operational purposes.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal information; object to or restrict certain processing; or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also have the right to complain to a relevant data-protection authority. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. Children

SpareQuote is intended for business and general spare-parts sourcing use, not for children. The service is not knowingly designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

10. Third-party services and external links

The website or service may link to supplier websites or rely on third-party services. Those parties operate under their own terms and privacy practices. Review their policies before sharing information. Add a list of actual third-party providers before production launch.

11. International processing

Depending on where hosting and service providers operate, information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards should be implemented for international transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as SpareQuote evolves. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.
